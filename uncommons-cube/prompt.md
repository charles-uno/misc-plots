
# Cube Brainstorming

We are creating a cube in the game Magic: The Gathering. The cube is mostly made of uncommons.

## Emphasize the Uncommons

Uncommons are the most satisfying part of a booster draft. They're in the sweet spot between power and complexity. They're the enablers and payoffs for the set's featured themes and synergies. When a draft goes well, and all the pieces of your deck fit together perfectly, it's the uncommons that really shine. 

This cube leans into that feeling. No bombs that steal a game solo. No forgettable filler. Every card is a role-player that invites you to build your deck around it. There are a million different ways to connect the dots. 

## Card Selection

We sometimes fill an empty niche with a common or rare. But the target -- in terms of both power level and complexity -- is "pretty strong uncommon from a recent draft set". 

## Power Creep

The power level of MTG cards has consistently increased over the years. Most uncommons printed 5+ years ago are no longer suitable for the cube. They've just fallen too far behind compared to recent sets. 

This is particularly true of creatures. Noncreature spells have been less affected.

"Bomb" uncommons from old sets may still hold up. 

We may want to exclude "bomb" uncommons from very recent sets. They may eclipse too many otherwise-playable cards.
