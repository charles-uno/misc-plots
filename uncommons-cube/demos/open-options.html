MTG Uncommons Cube — Open Triad Options Stress-Test

12-card skeletons for all 13 open candidates across RGW, RWB, BRG, and GWU. Hover over any card name to preview it from Scryfall. Note: for BRG Option C, Caustic Exhale (MV=1) is also a great fit alongside Nighthowl Pursuer if two 1-drops are desired.

RGW Open Options

RGW Option A: Go Wide (1/1 Tokens, Convoke & Team Pump) STRONG
Holds up cleanly. Every color has deep token generation and team-wide payoffs across the curve, though RG's two-color signpost slot leans on Pack Tactics (Targ Nar) rather than pure token text.

RGW Option B: Tap/Untap Shenanigans (Saddle, Survival & Convoke) STRONG
Very cohesive thanks to OTJ/DFT Mounts, DSK Survivors, and EOE tap triggers. Red's tap payoffs are mostly Mounts/Pilots, so it plays like an aggressive creature-combo deck.

RGW Option C: Dragons & Behemoths (Dinosaurs / Dragons / Power 4+) SHAKY
Strains in White and RW. Even with Kinjalli's Caller and Osseous Exhale, White mostly wants to destroy power 4+ creatures rather than reward you for controlling them, and RW lacks an uncommon payoff that rewards big creatures/Dragons/Dinosaurs (Swathcutter Giant / Relentless Raptor are just standalone bodies).

RWB Open Options

RWB Option A: Little Guys (Power ≤ 2 Matter & MV ≤ 3 Recursion) MODERATE
White and WB/RW are fantastic (Arabella, Wispdrinker Vampire, Scout for Survivors), but mono-Red and mono-Black are thin on true uncommon payoffs: Red mostly grants unblockability to power ≤ 2, and Black's MV ≤ 3 text is mostly reanimation or removal rather than ongoing engines.

RWB Option B: Goblins / Fey Kindred (Goblin + Kithkin + Elf + Faerie + Dwarf) FALLS APART
Splits along the seams. Even if you batch Goblins, Kithkin, Elves, Faeries, and Dwarves into 'Fey' (or treat all 1/1 tokens as Goblins), WB has zero Fey-kindred signposts (Baron Bertram Graywater only cares about tokens generally) and the cards read like three separate Lorwyn tribes.

BRG Option C: Ferocious, Dragons & Behemoths (Power 4+ Matters) STRONG
